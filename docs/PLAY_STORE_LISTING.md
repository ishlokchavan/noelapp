WHY NOEL

FIND IT FAST

SAVE & ENQUIRE

LIST YOUR HOME, COMMISSION-FREE

You can browse everything without an account. Sign in with Google or email to sync your shortlist.

## Data safety form (Play Console → App content → Data safety)
Mirrors the App Store "App Privacy" answers already on file:

- **Does your app collect or share user data?** Yes.
- **Data types collected:**
  - Personal info → Email address — required for account creation, not shared with third parties.
  - App activity → App interactions — used for analytics/personalisation, not shared.
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes.
- **Can users request data deletion?** Yes — in-app at Profile → Delete account.
- **Is data collection optional?** Yes — browsing is fully anonymous; only signing in requires an email.
- **Do you use data for advertising or share with third parties?** No.

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)
Answer "None"/"No" to all violence, sexual content, gambling, and controlled-substance questions — same as the 4+ Apple rating. Expect **PEGI 3 / Everyone**.

Note: the app has user-generated content (property listing photos/descriptions submitted by sellers, reviewed before publishing) — declare this in the UGC section, same as done for Apple's 2.3.6 requirement, since sellers upload their own photos.

## Target audience & content
- Target age group: 18+ (real estate transactions aren't aimed at children).
- Ads: No ads in the app.

## App access (if reviewers need to sign in)
"Listings are fully browsable without an account. To test sign-in, use Google or create an email account on the Profile tab. Account deletion is available in-app at Profile → Delete account. Enquiry buttons open WhatsApp/Phone, which won't function in an emulator without those apps installed."
